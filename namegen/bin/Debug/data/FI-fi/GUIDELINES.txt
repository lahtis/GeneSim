# GENERAL DATA PROTOCOL / NAME GENERATION RULES (v1.1)
# This document defines how historical eras and data columns are structured.

[COLUMNS / PERIODS]
Use the --period [number] flag to select the historical era.
1 | 1850-1860 | Biblical and Swedish tradition (Pre-Fennoman era)
2 | 1860-1870 | Rise of the Estates and Swedish-speaking gentry
3 | 1870-1880 | Classical era (The Gripenberg / Upper-class influence)
4 | 1880-1890 | National Romanticism and the Fennoman movement
5 | 1890-1900 | Industrialization and early Finnicization of surnames
6 | 1900-1910 | Pre-independence era and Modernism
7 | 1910-1920 | Early years of Finnish Independence

[DATA INSTRUCTIONS]
- Encoding: Use UTF-8 (without BOM) for Finnish characters (ä, ö).
- Format: Use Comma-Separated Values (CSV).
- Logic: The program reads the name from the selected column.
- Fallback: If the selected column is empty, the program uses the nearest available data from previous columns.

[CULTURAL NOTE]
Periods 1-3 lean heavily towards Swedish and Latin influence. 
Periods 4-7 represent the transition towards purely Finnish linguistic roots.
